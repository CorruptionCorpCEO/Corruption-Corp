Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Short version: we collect as little as we can. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking cookies. You can delete your account and what's tied to it at any time from your profile.

This policy covers corruptioncorp.net, including the programs hosted on it (Pinballa, Fractal Crack and MixPass). "We" and "us" means Corruption Corp. Questions go to hello@corruptioncorp.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address and password. To make an account you give us an email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication). We never see or store them ourselves.
  • Signing in with Google. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address. Google also sends your name and profile picture. We don't use or display them, and your profile starts with a generated name instead. We never get your Google password.
  • Your public profile. The display name, profile picture and (in Pinballa) description you choose are public. Anyone can see them next to the things you make. Your email address is never shown publicly.

Things you make or send

  • Pinballa: machines you publish, your arcade high scores and your votes are public. Machines you save without publishing stay in your browser.
  • Grit (the graffiti wall): what you draw is public and becomes part of the shared wall.
  • Contact form: your name, your message and your account's email address, so we can reply.
  • Artist recruitment form: your stage name, location, occupation and what you write about yourself.
  • Media suggestions (Chaos members): the YouTube link and note you submit.

Only Corruption Corp can read contact, recruitment and media-suggestion submissions.

Corruption Corp Chaos (paid membership)

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store them. From Stripe we keep the status of your membership (for example active or cancelled), its billing dates, and the reference IDs needed to manage it.

Collected automatically

  • An "active today" marker. At most once a day, a signed-in account records the date it was last used. We use it only to count active users.
  • Drawing on Grit without an account. To stop the free drawing allowance being reset over and over, the site looks up your IP address through ipify, scrambles it into a one-way code (a salted hash), and stores only that code with your drawing allowance. We do not store your IP address itself. The site also creates an anonymous session for you; it isn't linked to your name or email.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address and browser details when pages are requested. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Stored on your own device

The site keeps a few small things in your browser's local storage so it remembers you: whether you're signed in, light or dark mode, player volume, Pinballa drafts and similar settings. They stay on your device and you can clear them through your browser at any time. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in across the site and its programs.
  • To show your public profile and the things you publish.
  • To reply to messages and review submissions.
  • To provide Chaos membership perks and manage billing.
  • To prevent spam and abuse (drawing allowances, one submission per person, and so on).
  • To send you occasional Corruption Corp news by email. Every email will include a way to unsubscribe, or you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the services that run the site for us:

  • Google Firebase: accounts, the database and server functions (Firebase privacy).
  • Google Sign-In: only if you choose to sign in with Google (Google privacy policy).
  • Stripe: Chaos payments.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting.
  • ipify: the IP lookup described above.

We may also disclose information if the law requires us to.

Embedded content

The Media channel and some artist pages play videos from YouTube, and the music player plays tracks from SoundCloud. When those players load, YouTube and SoundCloud may collect data and set cookies under their own policies: Google/YouTube and SoundCloud. Links to social media sites (Instagram, X, YouTube) are governed by those sites' own policies.

How long we keep it

  • We keep account information until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account (Profile → Delete account) permanently removes your login, your profile, your published Pinballa machines, your scores and votes, and your activity marker. Any active Chaos membership is cancelled first.
  • Contact, recruitment and media submissions are kept until we've dealt with them. Email us to have yours removed sooner.
  • Drawings on Grit stay part of the shared wall.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your display name and picture on your profile at any time, and delete your account there. You can also email hello@corruptioncorp.com to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it or to stop us emailing you. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under local law, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Corruption Corp is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase and protected by access rules, so only you can change your own account and only Corruption Corp can read private submissions. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll also post a notice on the site.
